Risk Assessment

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Sudden failures caused by old plumbing, poor waterproofing, or moisture nobody can see may lead to property damage, injuries, and expensive lawsuits. A Mandeville Restoration Co risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. You receive a written report that lists each risk, ranks it by priority, and spells out the preventive steps to take.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Insurance companies and courts tend to favor a property owner who acts on expert recommendations and has inspections done regularly by professionals. Every commercial property should have an annual moisture assessment, and high-risk areas, including aging plumbing systems, basements, and mechanical rooms, should be inspected more often.
